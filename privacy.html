Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile games developed by Justinas Mekšėnas, available on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

My games are general-audience games. I do not operate user accounts or directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data. However, some games use third-party services that may automatically collect information required to provide their services.

Third-Party Services

Depending on the game and platform, the following third-party services may be used:

Google AdMob

Some games display banner, interstitial, or rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information about advertising interactions. Personalized advertising may be used where permitted and based on your consent or applicable settings. For users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Google UMP may be used to provide required advertising consent choices. AdMob Privacy.

Google Analytics for Firebase

Some games use Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage and improve the games. Standard Firebase Analytics data may include app usage, device information, and identifiers. Firebase Privacy.

Firebase Crashlytics

Some games use Crashlytics to detect and diagnose crashes. Crash reports may contain technical information such as device model, operating system, app version, and crash details. Firebase Privacy.

Google Play Games

Android games may use Google Play Games for features such as leaderboards, achievements, and multiplayer. Google may process information associated with your gaming activity and Google Play Games account. Google Privacy Policy.

Apple Game Center

iOS games may use Game Center for leaderboards, achievements, and multiplayer. Apple may process information associated with your Game Center profile and gaming activity. Apple Game Center Privacy.

Multiplayer Features

Some of my games offer online multiplayer features. Information may be processed temporarily as necessary to provide these features and connect players. I do not use this information to create persistent user profiles.

Data Sharing and Retention

I do not sell or rent personal information, and I do not maintain a personal-information database for my games. Information processed by Google, Apple, Firebase, AdMob, or other third-party services is handled according to their respective privacy policies. These services may process or store information in countries outside your country of residence.

Children's Privacy

My games are intended for a general audience and are not specifically directed at children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children for my own purposes. Third-party services used by the games may have their own policies and age-related requirements.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, you may have rights regarding your personal data, such as access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or withdrawal of consent. Because much of the information associated with the games is processed directly by third-party services, privacy requests concerning that information may need to be made to the relevant provider.

If you have a privacy question or request, you can contact me using the email address below and I will do my best to assist.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy when my games, services, or applicable legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
